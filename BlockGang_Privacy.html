Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile game Block Gang ("the Game"), developed by TasoApps ("we", "us"), handles information. By using the Game you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

We do not ask for your name, e-mail address or any account, and we do not run our own servers. Your game progress (levels, stars, settings, daily challenge streak) is stored only on your device.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Game shows ads through Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may automatically collect and process:

  • the device's advertising ID,
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it,
  • device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version),
  • ad interactions (ads viewed or tapped) and diagnostic data.

Depending on your choices, ads may be personalized or non-personalized. Google processes this data under its own policies: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

3. Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the Game asks for your consent through Google's consent form before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy options inside the Game.

4. Notifications

The Game may send local reminders about the daily challenge. They are created on your device and contain no personal data. You can turn them off in your device's notification settings.

5. Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us and we will help remove it.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalization, in your device settings (Android: Settings → Google → Ads).
  • Uninstalling the Game deletes all game data stored on your device.
  • Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, KVKK or CCPA), you may have rights to access, correct or delete your data, or to object to its processing. Because we do not hold your personal data ourselves, requests about advertising data are best directed to Google; we will gladly help.

7. Security and retention

We do not store your personal data on our own systems. Data processed by Google is kept according to Google's retention policies.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The new version will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
