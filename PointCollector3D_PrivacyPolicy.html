Point Collector 3D Privacy Policy

Effective: 28 October 2026 App ID: apps.taso.PointCollector3D

Point Collector 3D does not have its own servers and the developer does not collect your personal data directly. Your game progress is stored on your device and, if you sign in, in your Google Play Games account. Ads are provided by Google AdMob, which collects some device data as described below.

Who we are

Point Collector 3D ("the game") is published on Google Play by TASO APPS (Tahsin Karakılıç), Türkiye. This policy explains what data the game and the third-party services it uses handle, and the choices you have.

Data stored on your device

The game saves the following on your device only: sound, music and vibration settings, your best score, the skin you selected, unlocked skins and how many ads you have watched toward each skin. This data is not sent to the developer. It is deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its data.

Google Play Games Services

When you open the game, it signs you in to Google Play Games automatically, if your device allows it. This is used to save your progress to the cloud so it is not lost when you change or reset your phone.

  • What is saved: best score, unlocked skins and ad progress toward locked skins.
  • Where: in the Saved Games area of your own Google account, managed by Google. The developer cannot see your Google account details.
  • Player ID: Google provides the game with your Play Games player identity so it can read and write your save.

You can delete this data, or your whole Play Games profile, from the Play Games app or from your Google Account. Google's handling of this data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows banner, interstitial and optional rewarded ads through Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects and shares with Google:

DataPurpose
Device identifiers (Android advertising ID, app set ID)Advertising, analytics, fraud prevention
Approximate location (estimated from IP address)Advertising, analytics, fraud prevention
App interactions (app launches, taps, ad views)Advertising, analytics, fraud prevention
Diagnostics (performance and SDK stability data)Analytics

See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Your ad choices

  • If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice later with the Privacy button in the game's Options menu.
  • On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID under Settings › Google › Ads (or Settings › Privacy › Ads, depending on your device).
  • Rewarded ads are always optional. You only watch them when you choose to, for example to get an extra life or unlock a skin.

Security

All data sent by the Google services used in the game is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS).

Children

The game is intended for users aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. The developer does not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided data, contact us and we will help remove it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or Türkiye's KVKK), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your data and to object to its processing. Because the developer does not hold your personal data, most requests are handled directly through Google: your Google Account, the Play Games app, and the Privacy button in the game. You can also contact us and we will help.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the game changes. The new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
